About

From data architecture to business transformation

I lead IT delivery at Florida Blue, where I've spent the last decade helping a large health plan modernize the systems behind enrollment, billing and the member experience. My focus is business transformation: aligning technology roadmaps with what the business actually needs, then getting teams to deliver it.

Before Florida Blue, I was a BI Solution and Data Architect at Citi, designing the global analytics program that brought the bank's internal data into common warehouses. I spent 11 years with Tata Consultancy Services before that, rising from business analyst to BI architect on programs for Citi, USAA and banks in India, after starting as a software engineer at Hughes Telecom in 1999.

I'm a TOGAF-certified enterprise architect with a B.Tech in Mechanical Engineering from the University of Calicut. That engineering start still shapes how I work: break the system down, find the constraint, fix it.
